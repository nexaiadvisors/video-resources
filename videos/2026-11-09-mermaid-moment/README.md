# From Puppet to Swimmer: My AI Mermaid in 5 Steps

Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EfLqzTyMI-M

**What you get:** a one-page checklist for making something new with AI when you don't quite know what you're doing yet, plus the copy-paste prompt from the video.

## Your mermaid moment: the 5 steps

1. **Make a rough first version fast.** Expect it to be wrong. That is the point of the first try.
2. **Show the AI a real example.** When the result is almost right, stop describing what you want.
3. **Trust your own eyes over the plan.** Try the clever ideas, but keep only what looks right to you.
4. **Lock what works.** Once something looks right, fix it in place so it can't drift: your logo, your prices, a character's shape.
5. **Watch every version yourself** before anyone else sees it. The software won't flag what looks fake. You will.

## The prompt

Copy this into ChatGPT or Claude, with your example attached:

> Here is a real example of what I want. Match it, keep everything else the same, and tell me what you changed.
