# The first prompt to give any coding agent

Copy and paste this the first time your agent works on the always-on machine:

> Connect to my always-on Mac by its name with your own key. Read its logs and settings before you change anything, and ask me before any change on that machine.

## Three rules

1. **The agent gets its own key.** Never give it your password.
2. **Your passwords stay with you.** When a step needs one, the agent hands that step to you.
3. **Nothing on the main machine changes without your yes.**
