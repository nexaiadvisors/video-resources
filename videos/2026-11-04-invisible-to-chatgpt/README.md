**What you get:** Free AI checker skill, plus the message to send whoever runs your website.

## The free checker
It asks ChatGPT your customers' questions, checks whether AI tools are blocked from your site, and lists what to fix. It works with Claude:
https://github.com/nexaiadvisors/claude-skills/tree/main/ai-crawler-audit

## The message to send whoever runs your website

"Quick question: are AI tools like ChatGPT blocked from reading our site? Can you check three things for me: (1) our robots.txt doesn't block GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot or Bingbot, (2) any 'block AI bots' setting at our host or Cloudflare is off unless we chose it, and (3) our main pages show their text without needing scripts to load. Thanks!"
